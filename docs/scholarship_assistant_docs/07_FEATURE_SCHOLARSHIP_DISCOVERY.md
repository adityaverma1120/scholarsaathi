# Feature Specification --- Scholarship Discovery Engine

## 1. Purpose

Help students discover scholarships relevant to their education and
circumstances without needing to know scheme names in advance.

## 2. Scholarship source categories

-   Central government schemes.
-   National Scholarship Portal schemes.
-   State government schemes.
-   University and college schemes.
-   Verified private foundation or corporate scholarships.
-   Other verified education support programmes.

Each source category must retain its provider identity. Do not present a
private scholarship as a government scheme.

## 3. Source hierarchy

Prefer: 1. Current official scheme guideline or notification. 2.
Official ministry/department/portal scheme page. 3. Official
institution/provider page. 4. Official application portal. 5. Secondary
discovery sources only as leads requiring official confirmation.

A third-party listing is not sufficient evidence for publishing an
eligibility rule or deadline.

## 4. Catalogue record requirements

-   Stable scheme ID.
-   Scheme name and aliases.
-   Provider and provider type.
-   Government level/state.
-   Academic year.
-   Scheme description.
-   Target education levels/courses.
-   Eligibility conditions.
-   Benefits and amount, if verified.
-   Required documents.
-   Application opening/closing dates.
-   Official application URL.
-   Official guideline URL.
-   Source publication/effective date.
-   Last checked date.
-   Reviewer and review status.
-   Version history.
-   Active, upcoming, closed, archived or unverified state.

## 5. Search and filtering

Allow filters for: - Domicile/state. - Education level. - Course. -
Scheme provider/type. - Fresh or renewal. - Application
open/closed/upcoming. - Category only where relevant. - Income band
where verified.

Search should support aliases and simple-language terms. Do not expose
sensitive profile details in URLs or analytics.

## 6. Discovery ranking

Ranking may consider: - Eligibility evaluation outcome. - Required
profile information completeness. - Application window status. -
Education/course relevance. - Geographic eligibility. - Source
verification quality.

Do not rank by payment amount alone. Do not bury an important "unknown"
condition. Show why a scheme appears in the list.

## 7. Coverage management

The catalogue must have an explicit coverage statement, such as
"selected verified schemes for academic year X". Avoid claiming
all-India coverage unless it has been established.

Start with a limited set of NSP and Uttar Pradesh schemes. Expand only
with a source collection and review process.

## 8. Updates and expiry

-   Scheme records must be academic-year aware.
-   A deadline from a previous year must never appear as current.
-   Expired records should be archived or clearly marked closed.
-   If a source page changes, create a review task.
-   If official information is unavailable, mark unknown.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

-   Every published scheme has an official source and verification date.
-   Every deadline and benefit is source-backed.
-   Search results show provider and academic year.
-   Expired schemes are not labelled open.
-   Unverified records are not presented as confirmed matches.
-   Students can open the official source from the scheme detail page.
