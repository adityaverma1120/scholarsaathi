# Product Vision

## 1. Vision statement

Build a trusted, accessible scholarship lifecycle assistant that helps
students discover relevant scholarships, prepare reliable applications
and understand what to do at every stage.

**Proposed positioning:** Your complete scholarship assistant ---
discover, check, prepare, apply and track.

The proposed name **ScholarSaathi** is a working suggestion only. The
product owner must approve the final name after checking domain,
app-store, trademark and repository availability.

## 2. Problem

Students may struggle with: - Finding scholarships spread across
central, state, institutional and private sources. - Understanding
complex eligibility rules and scheme-specific exceptions. - Knowing
which documents are required and whether they are current and
readable. - Detecting differences in names, dates of birth, parent
names, marks or other information across certificates. - Completing
online forms accurately. - Understanding application statuses such as
defective, pending, rejected, approved or payment pending. - Finding
authoritative correction and grievance routes.

These are product hypotheses that should be validated with student
interviews and observed application journeys. Do not claim measured
prevalence or impact without research.

## 3. Target users

### Primary users

-   Students in secondary and higher-secondary education.
-   Undergraduate and postgraduate students.
-   Students applying for central or state government scholarships.
-   Students applying for institution or verified private scholarships.
-   Renewal applicants.

### Supporting users

-   Parents or guardians assisting a student.
-   School or college support staff, only through clearly authorised
    workflows.
-   Platform administrators and scheme reviewers.

## 4. Core value proposition

A student maintains one profile and a private document collection. The
platform uses that information, with permission, to: - Find potentially
relevant scholarships. - Compare the student's information with
published criteria. - Explain matched, failed and unknown conditions. -
Identify documents needed for each scheme. - Flag possible document
issues before submission. - Guide the student through application
preparation. - Explain application progress and next steps.

## 5. Product pillars

1.  **Student profile:** Reusable, student-controlled academic and
    relevant personal data.
2.  **Document intelligence:** OCR, readability, completeness, field
    extraction and comparison.
3.  **Scholarship discovery:** Curated catalogue with source provenance
    and academic-year validity.
4.  **Eligibility intelligence:** Deterministic rule evaluation and
    transparent explanations.
5.  **Application readiness:** Scheme-specific document and field
    checklist.
6.  **Application assistance:** Guided form preparation with user
    review.
7.  **Lifecycle support:** Status explanations, correction guidance,
    grievance drafts and renewal support.
8.  **Conversational help:** Grounded AI explanations in plain language
    and supported languages.

## 6. Product boundaries

The platform is an independent assistance tool. Unless a formal
agreement and verified technical integration exist, it is not: - An
official government portal. - An official eligibility authority. - A
certificate issuing or authenticating authority. - A scholarship
sanctioning body. - A payment processor. - A representative that submits
or changes government applications. - A substitute for the current
official scheme guidelines.

Do not imply affiliation with NSP, a ministry, a state government, a
college or a private scholarship provider without written authorisation.

## 7. Success measures

Use real, privacy-conscious product analytics. Potential measures: -
Percentage of users who complete a profile. - Percentage of uploaded
documents successfully parsed. - OCR extraction confidence and
correction rate. - Number of cross-document warnings confirmed or
dismissed by users. - Percentage of scheme records with current source
verification. - Eligibility result distribution and rate of unknown
conditions. - Percentage of students who open official scheme
guidance. - Checklist completion rate. - User-reported usefulness and
comprehension. - Application preparation completion rate.

Never invent dashboard numbers. Do not present "scholarship approval
rate" or "money saved" unless the data is real, representative and
methodologically defensible.

## 8. Product principles

-   Accuracy and explainability over number of features.
-   Official source traceability over AI fluency.
-   Student consent and review before action.
-   No unnecessary collection of sensitive information.
-   Support low-end Android devices and slow connections.
-   Provide useful empty states when data is unavailable.
-   Treat uncertain results as uncertain, not as failures or passes.

## 9. Initial geography and coverage

Recommended starting scope: - National Scholarship Portal schemes that
can be verified from official scheme guidelines. - A deliberately
limited set of Uttar Pradesh schemes. - Expand to other states only when
reliable official rules, source links, academic-year applicability and
review capacity are available.

Do not advertise "all scholarships in India" until coverage has been
audited and maintained.
